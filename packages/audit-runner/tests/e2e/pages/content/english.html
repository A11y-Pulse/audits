Returns and refunds

Our phone lines are open Monday to Friday, 9am to 5pm.

Before you send anything back

Most items can be returned within 30 days of delivery, as long as they are unused and in their original condition.

Outdoor gear that has been used on a trip can still be returned if it is faulty. See the warranty section below.

- Keep the original packaging

- Include the returns slip from your parcel

- Remove any personal items from pockets

- Book a courier collection or drop it at a store

Rated comfort temperatures

-5 °C for the Ridgeline 2 with the winter fly.

-3 °C for the Summit 3 with the standard fly.

-1 °C for the Meadow 4 in sheltered sites.
